Scope

Public sources only, for every network, Discover included. Tools behind a login are described from public user guides, manuals, documentation, videos and filings, and the report says where nothing public shows them. Documents are used when anyone can open them without a login or paywall, including those marked confidential, and the source register records the marking.

How to read it

The strategy comes first, then one chapter per stage. Each chapter opens with its design moves, then compares how each network works for each partner type. Every sentence stated as fact links to its source in the register.
