International schemes

Eight schemes outside the United States, in alphabetical order. Four of them are alliance partners of Discover: JCB, UnionPay, RuPay and Elo {{cite:dgn-100}}. Three publish more of their economics than any US network does: Interac its switch fees, eftpos its interchange and scheme fee changes, and Cartes Bancaires its interchange {{cite:interac-011+eftpos-016+cb-015}}.
