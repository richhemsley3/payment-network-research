Reading the map

Friction levels rest on the evidence: severe means partners repeatedly lose money, time or control, or regulators and courts have stepped in. A differentiator is a move no incumbent offers well, that the evidence shows matters, and that would be hard to match quickly. Parity means incumbents already do it well or could match it within a release or two, so it is worth doing but gives no lasting edge.
