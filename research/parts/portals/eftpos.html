eftpos no longer has a site of its own. Its domain redirects into Australian Payments Plus, where the eftpos page carries tabs for businesses, consumers, interchange fees and support, states the merchant-choice-routing saving, and announces the surcharge rule change of October 2026. AP+ Developers is the developer surface for BPAY, eftpos and NPP together, with a self-service sign-up, and a note that users of the three portals it replaced must reset their passwords.
