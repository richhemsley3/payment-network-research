The site, walked

Diners Club International's own site speaks to cardmembers and to corporate clients. There is no issuer, franchisee, acquirer or developer door in its navigation. A bank that wants to issue is told to search for its country and to contact the Club, and a current issuer signs in through a ServiceNow login linked from the Discover Global Network site. Partner news lives on a separate stream, Club Connections, which is where the franchise model shows: new issuers in Bangladesh and the United Kingdom, an acquiring collaboration, fintech card launches {{cite:diners-w-site|observed September 25, 2026}}.
