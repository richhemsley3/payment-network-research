The portals, walked

The Discover family meets its partners through ten front doors, most with their own registration {{cite:dgn-041+dgn-024+dgn-196}}. The marketing site carries the catalogue and the contact form. The Developer Center is a gateway with a welcome line, a button that leaves for the Partner Product Portal, and an FAQ that says access comes by invitation. The Partner Product Portal lists twelve products with public specifications and a resources page that names the rest: the Release Compliance Tool for testing and certification, the Disputes Portal, EASI for acquirers and processors, Web Merchant Boarding. The site's own Log In lands on the Fraud and Risk Center, which lists four more client portals in a side panel. A stranger counts ten places to sign in before finding the one that is theirs {{cite:dgn-w-site|observed September 25, 2026}} {{cite:dgn-w-dev}} {{cite:dgn-w-partner}} {{cite:dgn-w-easi}}.
