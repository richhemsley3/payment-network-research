The site, walked

PULSE publishes the shape of a client relationship. There is no developer platform to publish. The site is organised by what it offers and by who the reader is, with a page each for issuers, ATM acquirers and merchants, and every one of those pages ends in the same support promise, "When you call, we answer." The client website sits behind a registration form, and the login page is the one place in this study that publishes a turnaround: credentials within five business days. Client Support lists a phone number, hours and named mailboxes for disputes, fraud and the processor portal without asking for an account {{cite:pulse-w-site|observed September 25, 2026}}.
