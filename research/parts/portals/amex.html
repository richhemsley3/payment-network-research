The portal, walked

American Express Developers is small and readable. Sixteen products sit in one menu, every product opens on an overview with a guide and an API reference that a stranger can read to the endpoint, the sample code and the version history, and the path is written on the landing in four steps that end with "get access" after sign-up. The portal reads as one product's documentation site for a closed network: the catalogue is a menu, the search is a product filter, and the support route is a ticket form with its hours stated {{cite:amex-w-dev|observed September 25, 2026}}.
