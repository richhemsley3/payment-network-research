Across the journey, the card networks run partner experiences through people, gated portals and daily files, and most of what matters sits behind a contract or a login {{rcite:visa-250+amex-038+mastercard-064+dgn-035+visa-555+mastercard-482+amex-041+dgn-164}}.

Visa and Mastercard lead on open developer sandboxes, published rulebooks and incentive budgets of 15.75 and 20.5 billion dollars in their 2025 years {{rcite:visa-190+mastercard-002+mastercard-171+visa-313+visa-267+mastercard-105}}.

The Discover family already holds pieces of a better experience, such as PULSE's one-page invoice and Debit Dashboard, a live host simulator and a light change load {{rcite:pulse-012+pulse-142+dgn-198+dgn-391+dgn-491+dgn-298}}.

The study infers that Discover cannot outbid incumbent incentives, so its opening lies in certainty the incumbents do not give: traceable money, one shared record and plain change {{rcite:dgn-383+dgn-293+cross-229+cross-379+unionpay-042+cross-452}}.
