Scope

Partners only. Cardholder portals, apps and complaints are out of scope. Nothing behind a login was used for any network, Discover included, and no account was created on any site.

How to read the labels

Verified means read on a primary document: the network's own docs, rules, fee schedules or filings, a regulator, a court, or the browser on the day. Reported means trade press, analysts, forums and third-party demonstrations. Vendor means a network's claim about itself with no outside check. Hover a citation for its label and date.
