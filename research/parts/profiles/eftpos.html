eftpos · operated by Australian Payments Plus since 2021 · issuers, acquirers, merchants, fintechs, developers

eftpos publishes the interchange schedule and lets the regulator set the frame. The schedule is public with current and legacy tabs by card and merchant category, strategic small-business rates fell to 2 cents in store and 3 cents online in December 2024, and issuer scheme fees were cut 22 percent in May 2025 {{cite:eftpos-014+eftpos-015+eftpos-016}}. From October 1, 2026 surcharging on eftpos ends under the Reserve Bank's conclusions, and the schemes and large acquirers must publish the fees they charge {{cite:eftpos-018+mastercard-120}}. The partner surfaces are three: a ServiceNow member portal, a brand portal and AP+ Developers, with consolidation on the roadmap {{cite:eftpos-010+eftpos-011}}.

OwnerAustralian Payments Plus, formed from eftpos, BPAY Group and NPP Australia with competition approval in 2021.{{cite:eftpos-001|AP+}}
ReachOver 70 million debit cards and over 50 million contactless multi-network cards. About 35 percent of online debit payments in July 2024.{{cite:eftpos-002+eftpos-003+eftpos-004|AP+, Vendor}}
MembersBanks, fintechs and retailers. Worldpay joined in July 2023 and J.P. Morgan Payments in July 2024.{{cite:eftpos-005|AP+}}
ServicesFraud prevention, tokenisation, EMV 3-D Secure, stand-in processing made optional in October 2025, Click to Pay announced, tap to pay on phones with named partners.{{cite:eftpos-006+eftpos-007+eftpos-009|AP+}}
DevelopersApps and API keys issued through AP+ Developers with self-service sign-up and a support mailbox.{{cite:eftpos-012+eftpos-013|AP+ Developers}}
MandatesA Click to Pay parity mandate from 2027, 3DES to AES migration by 2030, 3-D Secure 2.3. Scheme rules themselves are not published.{{cite:eftpos-021+eftpos-020|AP+ roadmap}}
