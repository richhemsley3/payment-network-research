STAR · owned by Fiserv · issuers, merchants and acquirers, ATM operators, sold with Accel and MoneyPass

STAR is a Fiserv product with a network's economics, and in 2026 it is for sale. Its public presence is one Fiserv page with brochures, its only contact is a phone line and a shared mailbox, and its pricing is two named programmes without figures {{cite:star-016+star-015+star-013}}. In July 2026 the Wall Street Journal and Reuters reported large banks exploring a purchase, an activist called for a review, and the new chief executive said the networks would be part of a company-wide review {{cite:star-017+star-018+accel-022}}.
