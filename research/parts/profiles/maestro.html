Maestro · Mastercard's PIN debit brand · issuers and merchants through Mastercard, retiring in Europe

Maestro is being retired in Europe and still counted in the United States. Since July 1, 2023 European issuers replace expired or lost Maestro cards with another product, and Mastercard's FY2025 filing still lists the brand without mentioning the phase-out {{cite:mastercard-137+cross-129}}. The Department of Justice describes Mastercard-branded U.S. debit cards as typically carrying Maestro plus an unaffiliated back-of-card network {{cite:cross-135}}. No public volume, card or transaction figure exists for the brand, since Mastercard excludes Maestro and Cirrus from its reported gross dollar volume {{cite:cross-131}}.
