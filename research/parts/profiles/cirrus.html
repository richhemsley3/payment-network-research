Cirrus · Mastercard's global ATM brand · ATM operators and issuers through Mastercard

Cirrus is a cash-access brand without a public number. Mastercard defines it as its primary global cash-access solution, switches substantially all cross-border Cirrus transactions and earns fees on them, and excludes the brand from its reported volume {{cite:cross-130+cross-132+cross-131}}. ATM operators meet it through Mastercard's ATM network rules, and the surcharge non-discrimination rule that governs it is in the same litigation as Visa's {{cite:mastercard-288+cross-141}}.

OwnerMastercard, listed as an active brand in the FY2025 filing.{{cite:cross-129|Mastercard 10-K}}
RulesCustomers may join other ATM networks as issuer or acquirer alongside the Mastercard ATM Network provided they are not competing ATM networks.{{cite:mastercard-072|Mastercard Rules}}
ATM interchangeMastercard's U.S. ATM interchange paid to acquirers is public and tiered by the issuer's quarterly volume.{{cite:mastercard-094|Mastercard}}
