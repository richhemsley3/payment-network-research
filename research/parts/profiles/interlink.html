Interlink · Visa's U.S. PIN debit network, operated as Interlink Network, Inc. · issuers and acquirers through Visa

Interlink is the back of a Visa debit card, with its own rules and its own schedule. The Department of Justice describes it as Visa's back-of-card network placed beside an unaffiliated network, and the complaint alleges Visa's issuer contracts discourage enabling card-present PINless, which keeps more transactions on the front of the card {{cite:cross-134+cross-035}}. Visa publishes an Interlink interchange schedule and an Interlink rulebook, and licenses the marks as part of the Visa marks licence {{cite:cross-143+visa-183+cross-150}}.

InterchangeExempt consumer retail 0.80 percent plus 15 cents, regulated 0.05 percent plus 21 cents, with the one cent fraud adjustment for certified issuers.{{cite:cross-143+cross-144|Visa Interlink schedule}}
Market position, as allegedAll PIN networks together, Interlink and Maestro included, carry about 11 percent of U.S. debit transactions and 5 percent of card-not-present debit.{{cite:cross-136|DOJ complaint}}
