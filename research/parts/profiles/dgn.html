Discover sells a relationship and meets the partner through ten doors. The partnering page offers no application steps, fees or timelines and positions the network as consultative, with the contact form as the way in {{cite:dgn-036}}. Behind the form the family runs more partner portals than any other network in this study, each with its own login and registration, and the Developer Center is invite-only while the acquirer portal is self-registered {{cite:dgn-041+dgn-034+dgn-023}}. The network is now Capital One's, its 2025 debit reissue has more than doubled network volume, and the filing groups the three brands as the Global Payment Network {{cite:dgn-087+dgn-056+dgn-120}}.

How a partner gets in

The contact form is the front door and it asks who you are before anything else, from nine U.S. partner types {{cite:dgn-035}}. Acquirers and processors register for EASI, which is open to eighteen named company types through a multi-stage application that collects contact, company, signing-authority and business-purpose details before credentials are emailed {{cite:dgn-032+dgn-033}}. Developers cannot register. New users submit the contact form and wait for a representative, and collaborators are added by invitation {{cite:dgn-002+dgn-003}}. After approval a partner gets an organisation and an application with credentials, an API Plan that mirrors the signed service agreement and sets the rate limits no page publishes, and a second factor in the form of a consumer application certificate that can be downloaded once {{cite:dgn-013+dgn-011+dgn-014}}. PULSE participants sponsor merchants, processors and ISOs and carry liability for them, under agreements that auto-renew without fixed terms {{cite:dgn-026+dgn-027+dgn-028}}. Diners Club issuing is handled by outreach to a brand mailbox, with no published steps {{cite:dgn-039}}. Fintechs are assembled through BIN sponsors, processors and program managers and are not contracted directly {{cite:dgn-037+dgn-133}}. Device and product vendors pass Discover type approval and appear in a public Partner Directory last updated May 21, 2026 {{cite:dgn-038+dgn-181}}.

What is sold

The public catalogue is organised by theme, with no partner-type view: enable payments, fraud and security, PCI compliance, issuing and co-creation, technology platforms, debit and ATM {{cite:dgn-123}}. The technology layer is D-PAS and D-PAS Connect for EMV, Discover Digital Exchange for wallets and tokens with developer APIs, and Discover Commerce Xchange {{cite:dgn-127+dgn-132}}. Stored Token Services holds merchant-bound and wallet-bound tokens and is enabled through a certified partner or directly {{cite:dgn-129+dgn-130}}. Fraud products are Enhanced Decisioning, ProtectBuy and a High Brand Risk programme that adds registration duties for acquirers in named verticals {{cite:dgn-125+dgn-118}}. PULSE sells issuers five things, including gateway access to STAR, NYCE, Interlink, Maestro, Plus and Cirrus from one connection, a full-service debit processing offer covering Discover, Mastercard and Visa programmes, and Discover Debit for regional banks and credit unions {{cite:dgn-138+dgn-140+dgn-141+dgn-139}}. ATM acquirers get the domestic network and exclusive access to Discover and Diners Club cards {{cite:dgn-142}}. The security programme is DISC, with a PCI relief scheme for merchants above 75 percent EMV, and Discover is a founding member of the PCI council {{cite:dgn-111+dgn-114+dgn-109}}.

Fees, seen from outside

Discover says it competes for partners on incentives, rebates, issuer fees and switch fees, and publishes none of the amounts {{cite:dgn-071}}. Alliance partners pay network assessment, discount and interchange fees {{cite:dgn-069}}. PULSE switch fees are per-transaction amounts in participation agreements, with fixed-term pricing for some {{cite:dgn-070}}. The Federal Reserve found Discover misclassified consumer cards as commercial from 2007 to at least 2023, overcharging merchants, and the counterparty restitution liability stood at 1.2 billion dollars {{cite:dgn-082+dgn-075}}. A merchant class settlement over the misclassification received final approval on May 20, 2026 {{cite:dgn-053}}. Discover reports, as a risk, that Visa's arrangements with merchants and acquirers discourage routing debit to PULSE {{cite:dgn-074}}.

Support, as published

Support is segmented by role and mostly by mailbox: partnership, fraud solutions, interchange, and a single address for partners outside the United States and Canada {{cite:dgn-153+dgn-154}}. Processors have a named Account Executive as their escalation point, and issuers separate toll-free numbers for credit, debit and prepaid {{cite:dgn-148+dgn-150}}. Day-to-day operations for acquirers run through EASI's Message Center {{cite:dgn-155}}. Data compromises are reported by phone within 48 hours {{cite:dgn-152}}. There is no partner academy. The PCI training page points to the PCI council's programmes {{cite:dgn-157}}. PULSE publishes hours for its sales line and a two-business-day callback on web requests, and names help desks by persona {{cite:dgn-158+dgn-159}}. Discover positions the relationship as co-creation with network experts, and no ticketed desk is offered {{cite:dgn-161}}.

Twenty-four months

The Federal Reserve and the OCC approved the merger on April 18, 2025, with a consent order and a 100 million dollar fine over interchange overcharges, and the FDIC's parallel settlement carried a 150 million dollar penalty {{cite:dgn-042+dgn-043+dgn-084}}. The deal closed on May 18, 2025 and three Discover directors joined Capital One's board {{cite:dgn-046+dgn-048}}. Jason Hanson leads the Global Payment Network on Capital One's executive committee, with senior transfers from Capital One into acceptance and network roles {{cite:dgn-049+dgn-050}}. By February 2026 Capital One had substantially completed reissuing its legacy debit cards onto the network {{cite:dgn-055}}. In April 2026 trade press reported more than a hundred Discover application engineering jobs eliminated in the integration {{cite:dgn-058}}. The Advanced Payments Portal login now carries a Capital One acquisition notice, and the SCIP form is still issued under DFS Services LLC {{cite:dgn-178+dgn-121}}.
