What is sold, to whom

Issuers are offered debit payments and transfers, processing and gateways, ATM access, fraud detection and the Discover Debit programme, a signature debit product for community banks and credit unions sold on an all-in fee with a one-page invoice and an ABA endorsement, at a fee level the site does not state {{cite:dgn-138+pulse-034+pulse-012}}. The processing offer is full-service signature debit for Discover, Mastercard and Visa programmes {{cite:pulse-031}}. ATM acquirers get the domestic network, PULSE issuers and international partner networks, exclusive access to Discover and Diners Club cards, and a surcharge-free tie to MoneyPass {{cite:pulse-035}}. Merchants get PIN debit, PINless, e-commerce and mobile with routing choice {{cite:pulse-036}}. The Side-by-Side token service for card-on-file debit is enrolled by contact form and has no published specification or fee {{cite:pulse-005}}. PULSE markets API access and tokenisation to issuers and reaches no developer portal, reference, sandbox or SDK logged out {{cite:pulse-006}}.

Support and advocacy

Support is segmented by partner type with named hotlines and mailboxes, and Partner Support Services bundles the Debit Dashboard, DebitProtect, the Marketing Hub, full disputes support and research and advocacy {{cite:pulse-037+pulse-033}}. The sales line keeps weekday Central hours and web requests get a two-business-day callback {{cite:dgn-158}}. The dispute framework is not named publicly {{cite:pulse-015}}. The annual Debit Issuer Study is gated behind a form with its headline figures public: debit transactions up 4.6 percent, card-not-present at half of debit spend {{cite:pulse-019+pulse-041}}. PULSE sits on EMVCo, the U.S. Payments Forum, the Faster Payments Council, the FIDO Alliance and the Merchant Advisory Group {{cite:pulse-042}}.

Twenty-four months

The network changed owner in May 2025 and Capital One's filing groups it into the Global Payment Network without a separate volume {{cite:pulse-020+pulse-022}}. The filing flags that PULSE merchants are largely not contractually committed and that competitor routing strategies can hurt its volume, a Regulation II risk {{cite:pulse-023}}. PULSE claims Regulation II compliance for card-not-present routing through PULSE PAY Express {{cite:pulse-014}}. The careers link now points to Capital One {{cite:dgn-105}}.
