Mastercard Incorporated · Purchase, New York · more than 220 countries and territories in the filing, more than 210 on its own site · issuers, acquirers and processors, merchants, service providers, network enablement partners, fintechs, developers

Mastercard runs a franchise and a developer platform, and connects them by company ID. Every participant is approved as a Customer, licensed and assigned an ICA number and a Business Relationship Type, and that type is what decides which of the ninety-one developer products a company may take to production {{cite:mastercard-048+mastercard-059+mastercard-077}}. The developer side is the most open in this study: every documentation page has a markdown twin, the specifications download logged out, sandbox credentials are self-service, and a public status page reports each API's health {{cite:mastercard-001+mastercard-023+mastercard-002+mastercard-040}}. The franchise side is the most gated: rule changes are announced only inside Mastercard Connect, and the client portal is a hard login wall {{cite:mastercard-180+mastercard-037}}.

How a partner gets in

A Principal or Affiliate must be a regulated financial institution, meet financial requirements Mastercard sets and may assess subjectively, and hold AML and sanctions programmes as a condition of the licence {{cite:mastercard-049+mastercard-054+mastercard-055}}. The application is a public Qualtrics form titled Universal Onboarding Template {{cite:mastercard-082}}. Since May 20, 2025 a Principal needs Mastercard's approval before sponsoring a program manager, and Mastercard stopped designating new Type I processors on the same day {{cite:mastercard-051+mastercard-062}}. Third parties do not register themselves. The sponsoring customer registers each service provider in My Company Manager on Mastercard Connect, pays an initial and an annual fee whose amounts are not public, and carries the liability {{cite:mastercard-063+mastercard-064+mastercard-057}}. Operationally significant providers can contract directly as Network Enablement Partners, in tiers from Core to Platinum with a light package for small payment facilitators, and some roles must {{cite:mastercard-067+mastercard-068+mastercard-261}}. Fintechs are sold Product Express, where card issuance runs through a BIN sponsor and pre-certified processors {{cite:mastercard-274+mastercard-275}}, and Engage, a free marketplace of qualified enablers with a public directory {{cite:mastercard-277+mastercard-330}}.

What is sold, and what is mandatory

Value-added services were about 11 billion dollars in 2024 with a high-teens growth target, sold to fintechs, payment facilitators, acquirers, issuers, governments, small businesses and large merchants, and distributed through platforms, integrators, processors and other networks {{cite:mastercard-290+mastercard-291+mastercard-292}}. For issuers the catalogue is tokens (MDES and its Token Connect and customer-service APIs), processing, stand-in, network fraud scoring, Click to Pay enablement and loyalty {{cite:mastercard-240+mastercard-241+mastercard-243+mastercard-244+mastercard-247}}. For acquirers, MATCH Pro is mandatory before signing a merchant, its legacy files were decommissioned on January 1, 2026, and Onboard Risk Check is offered as API and as a screen inside Connect {{cite:mastercard-254+mastercard-255+mastercard-256}}. Disputes run through Mastercom, the mandatory channel, with a pre-chargeback collaboration step, a next-generation Extended API still migrating, and a first-party-trust liability shift for merchants with evidence {{cite:mastercard-210+mastercard-213+mastercard-216+mastercard-269}}. Money movement is Mastercard Send and Move, available only to or through a licensed acquirer or issuer {{cite:mastercard-280+mastercard-080}}. On the acceptance side, Mastercard Gateway and the 2025 Merchant Cloud bundle sit outside the switch, and Agent Pay launched in 2025 on top of tokens and disputes {{cite:mastercard-257+mastercard-258+mastercard-273}}.

Fees, seen from outside

Mastercard sets default interchange and disclaims any role in acquirer-to-merchant pricing {{cite:mastercard-262}}. The four network fee streams are named in the filing, and 2025 gross assessments ran to 11.0 billion domestic, 12.0 billion cross-border and 15.9 billion transaction processing {{cite:mastercard-103+mastercard-104}}. Canada publishes the acquirer assessments: 0.090 percent on volume and 1.00 percent cross-border {{cite:mastercard-095+mastercard-096}}. The U.S. ATM interchange paid to acquirers is public and tiered by issuer volume {{cite:mastercard-094}}. Developer terms describe paid API subscriptions billed monthly, quarterly or yearly, but no rate is published {{cite:mastercard-125+mastercard-033}}. The UK regulator's findings on scheme fees apply to Mastercard as to Visa, and a November 2025 revised U.S. merchant settlement replaced one the court had denied {{cite:mastercard-115+mastercard-109}}.

Rules and mandates in the window

Noncompliance runs in three categories, with Category A assessments from 25,000 dollars to 100,000 per violation within twelve months, an appeal to the Chief Franchise Officer within thirty days, and variances requested only through a tool on Connect {{cite:mastercard-186+mastercard-187+mastercard-189+mastercard-190}}. Account data compromises must be notified within 24 hours {{cite:mastercard-199}}. Mandates in the window include the end of undefined authorisations in June 2025, transaction link identifiers in October 2025, a stablecoin transaction type in April 2026, near-real-time age verification in June 2026, a card-level spend score in October 2026, and monitoring that moves from merchant to sub-merchant identifiers in April 2027 {{cite:mastercard-348+mastercard-341+mastercard-346+mastercard-342+mastercard-344+mastercard-354}}. Practitioners confirm the bulletins reach only member institutions through Connect {{cite:mastercard-353}}.

Support, as published

Urgent partner inquiries go to Customer Technical Services by phone and the rest to a Support Case Management application on Connect {{cite:mastercard-183+mastercard-318}}. On the developer side a ticket needs sign-in, the forum is public with four categories, and implementation help is routed to Engage partners {{cite:mastercard-041+mastercard-304+mastercard-238}}. The forum also records friction: sandbox access under review, a 403 from a pending sandbox, a developer asking how to load sample claims {{cite:mastercard-042+mastercard-043+mastercard-044}}. Training is Mastercard Academy, by region, whose landing shows selectors and no public catalogue {{cite:mastercard-088+mastercard-297}}. A Dispute Resolution Management team trains issuers, acquirers and processors on chargebacks {{cite:mastercard-230}}.

Twenty-four months

Domestic switching in China began in 2024 through the NetsUnion joint venture {{cite:mastercard-160}}. Maestro's European run-off continued, with issuers replacing expired cards with Debit Mastercard since July 2023 {{cite:mastercard-137}}. Merchant Cloud launched in 2025, Agent Pay in 2025, and Recorded Future became a Mastercard threat-intelligence service {{cite:mastercard-258+mastercard-273+mastercard-141}}. On the developer platform, FAPI 2.0 OAuth arrived in May 2026, automatic production mTLS certificates in June 2026 and a project history audit trail in July 2026, alongside a public MCP server and an agent toolkit {{cite:mastercard-311+mastercard-034}}. About 130 crypto co-brand programmes ran on the network in 2025 {{cite:mastercard-170}}. The FY2025 filing names Discover as a competitor now owned by Capital One {{cite:mastercard-162}}.
