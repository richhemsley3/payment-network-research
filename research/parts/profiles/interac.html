Interac Corp. · Toronto · Canada's domestic debit and transfer network · financial institutions, acquirers, connection service providers, fintechs, developers

Interac publishes its wholesale prices to the tenth of a cent. The switch fee on a point-of-sale debit transaction is 1.3985 cents, rising to 1.4438 cents on November 1, 2026, and the e-Transfer connection fees are 50,000 dollars once and 20,000 a month for a direct participant {{cite:interac-011+interac-015}}. Interac sets interchange and does not receive it, and calls the switch fee cost recovery {{cite:interac-012}}. The developer surface is by request per solution, immediate for identity products and conditional on an issuer relationship for payments {{cite:interac-021}}.
