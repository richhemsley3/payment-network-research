girocard · Germany · owned by the four banking associations of Deutsche Kreditwirtschaft, run by EURO Kartensysteme · issuers, network operators, acquirers, merchants

girocard admits network operators by contract and publishes the contract. The thirteen-page sample admission agreement with its technical annex and model merchant terms is public, the admitted-operator list names eighteen companies including Adyen, Stripe, Nexi and PAYONE as of March 2026, and the specifications sit behind a request-access login {{cite:girocard-023+girocard-007+girocard-016}}. Merchant fees are negotiated under the EU debit cap of 0.2 percent {{cite:girocard-022}}. There is no developer centre, and test cards are ordered from four card manufacturers {{cite:girocard-021}}.

Owner and operatorThe four associations of Deutsche Kreditwirtschaft as contracting owner. EURO Kartensysteme, Frankfurt, as operative scheme manager since 2023.{{cite:girocard-002+girocard-003+girocard-009|DK, EKS}}
Scale, 2025About 100 million cards, 8.3 billion transactions, 308 billion euros of turnover. 1.4 million active terminals in mid-2026, 92 percent contactless.{{cite:girocard-004+girocard-005|girocard statistics}}
AdmissionNetwork operators apply to DK and prove compliance with the technical annex under a published approval scheme. New operators and pilots start with EKS's market team.{{cite:girocard-019+girocard-020|DK, EKS}}
Partner surfacesDK Depesche circulars and interface specifications behind logins, a styleguide, model merchant terms, a product-development portal that renders only a login.{{cite:girocard-015+girocard-017|girocard.eu}}
Issuer servicesEKS sub-licenses German institutions as Mastercard affiliates under its own principal membership, and runs a loss-recording and prevention system for over 1,100 banks.{{cite:girocard-012+girocard-013|EKS}}
Twenty-four monthsA new admission contract edition in January 2025, a new styleguide in April 2026, and an in-app payment pilot announced June 24, 2026.{{cite:girocard-026+girocard-014|girocard.eu}}
