Plus · Visa's global ATM brand, operated as Plus System, Inc. · ATM operators and issuers through Visa

Plus is a marks licence and an ATM rule set. An issuer adds Plus by licensing the marks and providing round-the-clock authorisation, any member-owned, leased or sponsored ATM may join the Visa Global ATM Network, and non-bank deployers participate through acquiring partnerships {{cite:cross-147+cross-148+cross-128}}. The rule that ATM operators may not surcharge a Visa or Plus transaction more than others is in active litigation, with Cardtronics filing an opt-out complaint in August 2025 {{cite:cross-142+cross-141}}.
