Jeanie · a Worldpay network, now under Global Payments · issuers and ATM operators through Worldpay

Jeanie exists in the Federal Reserve's table and almost nowhere else in public. The network has no working website of its own, Worldpay's developer portal does not mention it, and trade-press coverage in the window is a single column {{cite:jeanie-014+jeanie-015+jeanie-016}}. Its owner changed on January 12, 2026 when Global Payments completed its acquisition of Worldpay {{cite:jeanie-004}}.
