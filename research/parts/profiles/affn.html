Armed Forces Financial Network · St. Petersburg, Florida · co-owned by two trade associations · military banks and credit unions, ATM and POS acceptance near bases

AFFN is a network owned by its members' associations, and it publishes the community and not the terms. Founded in 1985 at the Army's request, it is co-owned by the Association of Military Banks of America and the Defense Credit Union Council, and its site carries grant programmes, testimonials and executive names while the only rule document is the brand manual {{cite:affn-001+affn-002+affn-009+affn-011}}. The site sits on an FIS address and an FIS theme, and NYCE's cardholders reach base ATMs through it {{cite:affn-004+affn-005}}.

OwnerAMBA and DCUC, the two trade associations. DCUC represents over 180 credit unions.{{cite:affn-002+affn-007|AFFN, DCUC}}
ScaleAbout 346 institutions, 800,000 ATMs and 2.3 million point-of-sale locations in third-party figures from 2018. affn.org publishes none.{{cite:affn-006|Reported}}
Message modelA PIN-based, single-message POS system, and a single-message network in the Federal Reserve's table.{{cite:affn-008+jeanie-008|AFFN, Federal Reserve}}
ProgrammesA matching grant of up to 1,500 dollars per participant, in its twenty-third year, and support for the Veterans Benefits Banking Program.{{cite:affn-009+affn-018+affn-010|AFFN}}
Support and eventsOne phone line and general mailbox, named executives by department, and the co-owners' conferences as partner events.{{cite:affn-014+affn-015|AFFN}}
