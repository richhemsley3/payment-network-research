Diners Club International · a licensee network inside Discover Global Network · owned by Capital One since May 18, 2025 · franchise issuers in 35 to 44 countries, acquirers through Discover, corporate clients through local issuers

Diners Club is a franchise, and the network owner issues nothing. Outside the United States the licensees issue the cards and set their terms, so the partner-facing product is a licence per market, exclusive in most and non-exclusive in some {{cite:diners-002+diners-004+diners-025}}. Everything a licensee then needs sits inside Discover's portals: EASI for acquirers and processors, a ServiceNow site for current issuers, the Developer Center by invitation {{cite:diners-034+diners-042+diners-035}}. The public record on Diners is thin. Most of what can be read is the brand's own claims, which this profile labels as such.

What is sold, to whom

To issuers, Diners offers premium, co-branded, prepaid and debit consumer products, commercial programmes with expense management, and the loyalty layer the brand is known for: lounges, Destinations content and privileges {{cite:diners-026+diners-027}}. The commercial set of SMB, corporate and multinational cards with virtual card numbers and corporate travel accounts is delivered through local issuers, and the Tracker reporting tool is sold as a PDF {{cite:diners-028}}. Merchants accept Diners through their existing acquirer, and the brand's site offers a find-an-acquirer page and no application {{cite:diners-008+diners-006}}. Becoming an issuer is a contact form or a brand mailbox {{cite:diners-029+dgn-039}}. Device vendors go through Discover type approval into the public Partner Directory {{cite:diners-009}}.

Support, as published

Cardholder support goes to the local franchise, and the network's help centre is a country selector {{cite:diners-039+diners-040}}. Business enquiries from the Diners site land on Discover Global Network's shared contact form {{cite:diners-041}}. Current issuers work in a ServiceNow-hosted portal, acquirers and processors in EASI's Message Center {{cite:diners-042+diners-043}}. A public news stream, Club Connections, carries the partner announcements with dates and needs no login {{cite:diners-037}}. An online dispute resolution site exists at odr.dinersclub.com without saying who it is for {{cite:diners-038}}.

Twenty-four months

The network changed owner in May 2025 {{cite:diners-044}}. Since then the franchise has grown at the edges: Nium and Pax2pay issuing virtual cards for travel, BRAC Bank's Emerald card in Bangladesh, Moorwand adding UK acquiring to its issuing licence in December 2025, Sparkasse Pay's co-badged Diners and Mastercard card in Slovenia, Uzcard's alliance for acceptance across Uzbekistan, and ISX Financial's flykk debit card in the EU and UK on September 24, 2026 {{cite:diners-045+diners-046+diners-047+diners-048+diners-050+diners-049+diners-011}}. The brand calls itself the fastest-growing global network by alliance count {{cite:diners-053}}.
