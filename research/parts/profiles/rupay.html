RuPay · operated by NPCI, taken abroad by NPCI International · banks as issuers and acquirers, fintechs, alliance networks

RuPay is a policy instrument as much as a network, and its partner record is mostly Reported. NPCI's site blocked the study's fetches, so what follows rests on secondary sources and Discover's alliance pages. The merchant discount rate on RuPay debit was set to zero by government mandate from January 2020, businesses above a turnover threshold must offer RuPay debit, and credit on UPI has been permitted since 2022 {{cite:rupay-011+rupay-014+rupay-015}}. The Discover alliance dates from March 2012 and gives RuPay Global cards acceptance on Discover, Diners Club and PULSE {{cite:rupay-007+rupay-008}}.

OwnerNPCI, founded 2008 under the Payment and Settlement Systems Act with widened bank shareholding since 2016. NPCI International launched August 2020.{{cite:rupay-002+rupay-003+rupay-004|Reported}}
ScaleAbout 753 million cards from nearly 1,158 banks by November 2020, and 34 percent of card transactions by March 2021, in the last figures found.{{cite:rupay-005+rupay-006|Reported}}
PricingA historic flat switching fee of 60 paise to the acquirer and 30 to the issuer. An interchange circular for Singapore, the UAE and South Korea exists on the blocked site.{{cite:rupay-012+rupay-013|Reported}}
ServicesNetwork tokenisation through Token Reference On File since October 2021, PaySecure for e-commerce authentication, On-the-Go wearables.{{cite:rupay-016+rupay-017+rupay-018|Reported}}
Developer surfaceAn NPCI organisation on GitHub with a dozen repositories, none a RuPay kit. The Developer Studio on npci.org.in was not readable by the study's fetches.{{cite:rupay-023|GitHub}}
