NYCE Payments Network, LLC · an FIS company · issuers, acquirers and merchants through processors, ATM operators

NYCE is a product line of FIS, and the partner meets FIS. The network's own domain is now a cardholder site. For partners there is an FIS product page, whose calls to action are a contact form and the gated FIS client portal {{cite:nyce-017+nyce-016}}, and FIS's public API catalogue, which lists NYCE and PayNet APIs with a reference a stranger can read {{cite:nyce-027}}. FIS positions NYCE for banks and CULIANCE for credit unions as its two debit networks {{cite:nyce-009}}. Onboarding, as published, is issuer-side through the existing EFT processor with no reissue, and the study found no application steps, fees or certification stages in public {{cite:nyce-019}}.
