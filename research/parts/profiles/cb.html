Groupement des Cartes Bancaires CB · Paris · an economic interest grouping of French banks · principal members in two colleges, acquirers and payment institutions, vendors through certification

CB is a members' grouping with published interchange and a certification arm. The interchange rates in force since January 2021 are public, 0.20 percent on debit and prepaid, 0.30 on credit and 0.90 on commercial, and the grouping says they pay for clearing, settlement, the payment guarantee and collective security {{cite:cb-015+cb-016}}. Products reach the scheme through laboratory tests under the agrément programme run by its subsidiary PayCert, and acceptance providers through a two-tier labelisation {{cite:cb-013+cb-010+cb-014}}. The live site answered this study's browser with a block page, and the REMPARTS portal did the same for plain fetches, so the public pages were read from archived copies {{cite:cb-w-site+cb-028}}.

OwnerA French GIE created in 1984 by the main banks, owning PayCert, CB2i and FrenchSys and a stake in the STET clearing network.{{cite:cb-001+cb-005+cb-002|CB, CJEU record}}
Scale, 202315 billion transactions, 676 billion euros, 77 million cards, half of them contactless payments by count.{{cite:cb-003|CB key figures}}
MembersPrincipal members in two colleges, partner banks and others. Adyen becomes a principal member and a direct STET participant from April 1, 2026.{{cite:cb-012+cb-011|CB, Adyen}}
GovernanceA banks-and-industry advisory council twice a year and a commerce council with six merchant federations. A new chairman elected October 17, 2025.{{cite:cb-019+cb-025|CB}}
Merchant productsFAST'R authentication, SoftPOS on Android, Tap to Pay on iPhone, rental deposits, and dematerialised meal vouchers on the same terminals.{{cite:cb-007+cb-008|CB}}
Support and eventsA web form promising a reply as soon as possible, a training body CB2i Formation, and the annual Sommet CB with about 2,000 participants.{{cite:cb-022+cb-021+cb-023|CB}}
