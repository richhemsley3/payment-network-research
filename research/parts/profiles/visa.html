How a partner gets in

A bank applies for a licence through Visa Access after a regional team confirms eligibility, and the review runs on four axes: regulatory permissions, financial and compliance standing, and the rest {{cite:visa-004+visa-005}}. Everyone who is not a bank is an agent. A member must register every third-party agent through Program Request Management, per acquirer, after a senior officer's review and an on-site inspection {{cite:visa-008+visa-009+visa-010+visa-305}}. The published agent fee schedule dates from 2016, 5,000 dollars initial and annual for ISOs and payment facilitators, and the only published processing time is from the same FAQ, five to seven business days {{cite:visa-014+visa-015}}. Visa Partner now offers a self-service registration tool with three paths, ending in a business ID and a public directory listing {{cite:visa-161}}. Fintechs take Fast Track, which is eligibility criteria and enablement partners, with cost benefits described and no prices {{cite:visa-018+visa-266}}. Technology vendors take Visa Ready, and one track publishes both a timeline and a cost {{cite:visa-023+visa-024}}. ATM deployers are ISO-ATM agents, and an ATM must be owned or sponsored by a Visa or Plus client to qualify {{cite:visa-017}}.

What is sold, and what is mandatory

Value-added services were 10.9 billion dollars in fiscal 2025, sold in four portfolios through about 450 specialised sales staff {{cite:visa-071+visa-070+visa-074}}. On the issuing side the catalogue runs from processing (Visa DPS, Pismo) through tokens, risk scoring, disputes and stand-in authorisation to a Flex Credential {{cite:visa-075+visa-080+visa-082+visa-079+visa-089}}. Some of it is not optional. Account Updater enrolment is mandatory for issuers in most regions, alias directory participation is mandatory in three regions, and in Europe Visa auto-enrols every issuer in its authorisation risk score {{cite:visa-085+visa-086+visa-082}}. On the acquiring side the offer is the Visa Acceptance Platform under the Cybersource and Authorize.net brands, a merchant screening service that acquirers in three regions must use, and Tap to Phone with more than 20 million transacting devices {{cite:visa-093+visa-094+visa-101+visa-105}}. Disputes run through Visa Resolve Online, which merchants never log into {{cite:visa-098+visa-172}}. Money movement is Visa Direct, more than 12.5 billion transactions for more than 650 partners, and no fintech can take it to production without a licensed acquirer as sponsor {{cite:visa-056+visa-125}}.

Fees, seen from outside

Merchants do not pay interchange directly. They pay a negotiated discount to their acquirer {{cite:visa-248}}. Canada is the one market where Visa publishes its acquiring network assessment, 0.09 percent on all Canadian-acquired volume {{cite:visa-251}}. The Department of Justice complaint describes the debit side the schedules do not: per-transaction network fees, a fixed monthly acquirer fee since 2012, and routing contracts with volume cliffs {{cite:visa-274+visa-275}}. The UK regulator found core scheme and processing fees to acquirers rose at least 25 percent since 2017 and has now imposed transparency and pricing-governance directions on Visa and Mastercard {{cite:visa-278+visa-279}}. Since November 2025 a superseding injunctive settlement with U.S. merchants has been before the court {{cite:visa-270}}.

Rules and mandates in the window

Non-compliance follows a published ladder from a 1,000 dollar case fee upward, with Visa keeping full discretion to waive or deviate {{cite:visa-296+visa-297}}. The Acquirer Monitoring Program replaced three programmes in April 2025 with one count-based ratio, and enforcement began October 1, 2025 {{cite:visa-336+visa-337+visa-347}}. April 2026 brought rules for agentic transactions through digital wallets, a global scope for the member API-usage rule, and retirement of the separate Visa Direct processing guides {{cite:visa-318+visa-319+visa-320}}. From October 24, 2026 members must disclose every numeric identifier of each third-party agent {{cite:visa-304}}. Since April 18, 2026 any member using an API through the Developer Center is bound to its terms unless a bespoke agreement says otherwise {{cite:visa-167}}.

Support, as published

The developer platform publishes an email address, a contact form and an AI chatbot whose answers the terms say are not a communication with Visa {{cite:visa-350+visa-353+visa-356}} {{cite:visa-459}}. No phone, hours or response commitment is published {{cite:visa-353}}. Where no bespoke agreement applies, the terms say Visa has no obligation to support it {{cite:visa-354+visa-167}}. A non-member gets support from its sponsoring member {{cite:visa-355}}. Visa ran a peer-support developer community of 26,100 members, and its site did not answer on the day of reading {{cite:visa-365}}. The acquiring side is different: a one-to-two business day response to developer requests, a public status page, chat and cases behind sign-in, and more than twenty country phone numbers {{cite:visa-372+visa-376+visa-373+visa-375}}. Clients are assumed to have a named account executive, and the account model pairs a Client Success manager with a separate sales owner {{cite:visa-370+visa-386}}. Visa University sells paid training with exam-based certifications {{cite:visa-141+visa-382}}.
