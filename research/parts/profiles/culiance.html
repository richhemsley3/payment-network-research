CULIANCE · an FIS network for credit unions · issuers, credit-union ATM owners

CULIANCE is FIS's credit-union debit network, and it is not the one the study set out to find. The plan assumed CULIANCE was the former CO-OP network under Velera. The public record says otherwise: culiance.com carries an FIS copyright and routes credit unions to an FIS product page, while Velera's ATM network is still branded CO-OP and its debit network Pay Network, and neither mentions CULIANCE {{cite:culiance-002+culiance-006}}. Joining is an FIS sales enquiry with no published steps, fees or certification {{cite:culiance-013}}.
