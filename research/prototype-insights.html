What the network research means for the partner experience

The decisions the competitive study can inform about the Discover partner experience: where partners lose time or value today, which gaps to close, and which advantages are credible enough to press. Every insight was challenged in two rounds, and every sentence it states as fact cites its source.

Scope

Public sources only, for every network, Discover included. Nothing from any network's copy, figures or screens is meant for the prototypes. Visa and Mastercard appear as patterns and benchmarks.

How to read a card

Observed is what the sources show, cited sentence by sentence. The study infers is reasoning from those facts, labelled as such. Decision is the question it informs and what the evidence favours. Challenged is the strongest objection raised and how it was settled.

The decisions

Each decision is a question about the partner experience that the evidence can inform. The recommendation is what the cited insights favour, and the owner says who holds the lever. Where the business owns it, the experience can still make it visible, checkable and easy.

D1Should every fee and interchange line a partner sees trace to its transaction, merchant, card class and rule, with included services listed beside it?High confidence
The evidence favours
Yes, and design can build it now. The Fed found Discover charged a commercial rate it did not disclose and had no classification controls (Order to Cease). The consent order now requires those controls for each acquirer and merchant (Order to Cease). Add interchange and card class per transaction to Brightline's cycle breakdown, and open Clearway's variance row to its transactions. Keep each flag and its correction in one record. State one promise that the four services Discover and PULSE call free come at no separate charge, and list them on each statement (Discover Enhanced Decisioning · Fraud Management Tools · Fraud Detection and Risk · Discover Debit). Put a correction promise for every affected partner, and range data access terms, to the business and legal (Scheme Fees, Conclusions Paper · Global Payment Network IIN). Keep the enforcement history out of the story.
Serves
Close a major gap, less friction, competitive advantage and more value. Shared by the business and experience design.
Would change it
Sight of the consent order's compliance plans, which go to the Reserve Bank and are not public, could show partner-facing controls already exist (Order to Cease). A US rule requiring fees acquirers can reconcile would turn the trace into parity, and the study found none. Discover pricing for tokens or account updating would widen or narrow the inclusion promise. Partner evidence that the promise does not weigh in network choice would move it off the value proposition. Confidence is high for the fee trace (insight 2). The inclusion promise rests on a medium-confidence card (insight 13).

Gaps and advantages

Major gaps to shore up

  1. The US rate chart is confidential and card-class data needs an NDA, so partners cannot readily check the class behind a charge From 2007 until at least 2023, Discover classed millions of consumer cards as commercial, its highest tier for acquirers and merchants (Federal Reserve order 2025-10 · Discover 10-K 2024). It did not disclose that it charged them the commercial rate (Order to Cease). The Fed put the harm to acquirers and merchants near $1 billion (Order to Cease). Discover's US acquirer chart is confidential and opens only with an acquirer-issued code, and its fuller range table needs an NDA (U.S. Acquirer Interchange Pricing · Global Payment Network IIN). Trace each fee and interchange line to its transaction, merchant, card class and rule, machine-readable at no cost. Keep each flag and its correction in one record. Show an acquirer its standard rate and any incentive on separate lines. Give developers a card range lookup, with access terms set by the business. 2 · 9

Merchants likely pay more on the owner's migrated debit cards, and those on flat-rate or tiered plans cannot see it On a consultancy's quoted rate, a 50 dollar in-store sale costs 71 cents against 24.5 cents under the cap (Capital One x Discover · Capital One’s Discover Merger · Regulation II). On its figures merchants pay more unless their average in-store ticket is below 5.71 dollars (Capital One x Discover). Stripe says the interchange would likely be higher (Changes following Capital One's). A merchant services firm says merchants cannot route these cards differently, and that flat-rate and tiered plans bury the new rate (Capital One’s Discover Merger). Capital One lists a fall in large merchants accepting Discover or PULSE as a material risk (Capital One 10-K 2025). Drop family-wide lower-cost claims on debit, including the story's. Show each acquirer the share and cost of its debit on the new Discover ranges, with declines named by cause. Let Marcus see each acquirer's Discover debit share and cost, so he can raise the change first. 1

Advantages to press

  1. Large UK acquirers said they must buy extra services or consultancy from the schemes to understand their fees (MR22/1.10 Market review). Visa and Mastercard each told the UK regulator they could not give fee logic for all fees within a year (Decision on requests). The PSR deferred that duty only for fees earning £850,000 or less a year, a small share of fee revenue (Decision on requests). Acquirers can request that data meanwhile (Decision on requests). The UK direction covers scheme and processing fees and leaves out interchange (Specific direction 22). This map infers that a free trace of each line to card class and rule would go beyond what the UK requires of Visa and Mastercard. Lead the acquirer value claim with a statement its finance team can reconcile, each line tied to transaction, card class and rule. Once Discover confirms its controls, say who sets each card's class. Keep the enforcement history out of the story. 2

Pair it with the who-reads-each-fact view, since the same ownership opens the separation gap. Show sales from those cards per merchant once live. 1 · 14 · 6
